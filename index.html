About Ruth Kamau

Self Drive.

Ruth Kamau is a highly experienced and empathetic mediator and mental health professional based in Thika, Kenya. With a deep commitment to fostering peace and well-being, Ruth specializes in guiding individuals and families through complex challenges, offering both conflict resolution and compassionate counseling services.

Her approach is rooted in creating a safe, confidential, and supportive environment where clients can explore their concerns, develop effective coping strategies, and achieve sustainable solutions. Ruth's diverse expertise allows her to address a wide spectrum of issues, from family disputes and trauma recovery to personal growth and life skills development.

Dedicated to empowering her clients, Ruth combines professional insight with genuine care, helping them navigate life's complexities with resilience and clarity. Whether you're seeking resolution in a conflict or support for your mental well-being, Ruth is here to guide you on your journey to peace and wholeness. Visit her on TherapyRoute.com.

Developing effective communication and respectful manners is crucial for personal and social success. This aspect focuses on fostering polite and clear interactions.
